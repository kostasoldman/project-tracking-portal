Lesson Detail — Example (LL-014-01)

Missing Risk Register at project kickoff
Description
No Risk Register was created during Planning, so the team faced three critical issues with no mitigation plan in place.
What went right?
The team reacted quickly once the issues surfaced.
What went wrong?
No proactive risk identification — everything was handled reactively.
Recommendation
Always create a Risk Register at the kickoff meeting, with at least 10 identified risks.
